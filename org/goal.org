#+title: Goal

Current goal: implement the algorithmic operator-form generator described in
[[file:cft-code/basis-generation/operator-form-generation-plan.org][Operator-Form
Basis Generation Plan]].

Replace merged compact-mode traversal for OPE and printing with direct,
bounded =OperatorAtom= / =OperatorRecord= streaming.  Use family fragments,
weight and quantum bucket joins, and closed-string level joins to construct
the actual operator/state forms, not just counts.

The target is at least 10x speedup over the current compact Zig benchmark rows
while preserving enough structural output for OPE and state reconstruction:
seed id, seed body bitmask, source-tagged atoms, components, labels,
derivative orders, weights, and quantum values.

Constraint: keep the implementation data-oriented, streaming, memory bounded,
and minimal.  Do not trade the backend for large retained caches unless a
benchmark shows the memory cost is justified by a decisive algorithmic win.
